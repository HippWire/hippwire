We are inviting research collaborators to join HippWire.

HippWire is currently seeking research collaborators with background, expertise, or skills in one of the following areas.

Areas of expertise

  • Machine learning based medical and/or biological image segmentation
  • Hippocampal physiology, circuit neuroscience and/or connectomics
  • Theoretical and/or computational neuroscience

What characteristics we are looking for in our potential collaborators

  • Research taste and willingness to engage in REAL scientific discovery. Achieving SOTA on segmentation benchmarks is NOT the end of our research goal, we are going to process the REAL vEM data with the size ~400TB, and the processed results could be ~1PB.
  • Maturity and a sense of responsibility and ownership on the sub research topic engaged in.
  • Active research interest in hippocampus, memory circuits, or related systems
  • Willingness to make SOLID contributions to one of our sub research topics within shared standards
  • Commitment to open science and community data sharing
  • Ability to collaborate across labs and communicate findings clearly
